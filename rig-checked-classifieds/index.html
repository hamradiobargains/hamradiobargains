The Rig-Checked standard

Four things every listing has to show.

The standard is the product. A listing does not get to look trustworthy — it has to provide the information that makes trust reasonable.

1

Verified by postcard

Seller identity is verified with a code mailed to the address on the seller’s FCC license.

2

Rig Check on every listing

Every listing carries a condition report built from the questions that actually decide that model’s value.

3

Fair value in the open

HamRadioBargains’ fair market value is posted on every listing. The seller still sets his own asking price. You’ll see our fair value for your rig before your listing posts — no surprises after the fact.

4

Terms declared up front

Payment method and shipping terms are stated before a buyer has to ask, negotiate, or guess.

Why it’s different

Most classified boards wait. This one goes out and promotes the listing.

On a passive board, a seller posts an ad and hopes the right buyer eventually searches for it. Rig-Checked Classifieds is being built around the audience and distribution HamRadioBargains already has.

The passive-board model

The listing sits in one place while the seller waits for discovery.

  • Traffic depends mostly on search and habitual browsing
  • Ads often leave out the details buyers need most
  • Visibility may reward whoever pays for a boost
  • The seller does nearly all the work of being found

The Rig-Checked model

Complete, verified listings can earn active promotion instead of simply waiting to be found.

  • Selected listings can be featured in an Elmer’s Spotlight reel
  • The reel shows the rig, the seller’s verification, key Rig Check answers, price, fair value, and terms
  • Features can travel across HamRadioBargains channels
  • Sellers earn attention by representing their gear completely
Promotion is earned by complete representation. It is never bought, and it is not guaranteed to any listing. A postcard request also does not guarantee a Spotlight reel.

Questions

Before you request the postcard.

Is it live yet?

No — this is pre-launch. The sample board shows the intended experience, and postcard requests are the vote that tells us sellers want it built.

Does HamRadioBargains set my price?

No. Sellers price freely. Our fair market value is displayed on the listing regardless of the asking price.

What does the postcard prove?

It proves the person posting can receive mail at the address tied to the callsign on the FCC license.

What if my FCC address is out of date?

Update it with the FCC first, then request your postcard. We will not substitute a different mailing destination.
