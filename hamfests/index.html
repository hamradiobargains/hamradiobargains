Hamfests Worth the Gas

This isn't another hamfest calendar. These are the second-tier regional fests within a few hours of Raleigh, NC that I'd personally burn gas for — judged on flea-market and tailgate quality, table cost, and whether the used-gear hunting justifies the drive. The big famous shows don't need my help. These do. The fall swapmeet season just wrapped — the last fest on this list ran October 24 in Elgin, SC — so the list now leads with the 2027 editions to plan around.

Dates and prices: 2027 dates are shown where confirmed; prices are the most recent published figures and may move. When a detail says checking with the club, it means exactly that — confirm before you load the truck.

Charlotte Hamfest

Cabarrus Arena & Events Center, Gold Hall, Concord, NC · charlottehamfest.org

Elmer's take

An early-season indoor show about two and a half hours out. If you want another indoor option before spring really gets going, this is the one. Table pricing below is from the club's last published flea-market package (2024).

Flea market: Indoor flea market plus commercial vendors
Tables: $30 each, extra tables $20, electricity $45 (2024 published package)
Admission: $12 advance / $14–$15 at the door (2026)
Drive from Raleigh: ~2.5 hours
Saturday, April 3, 2027

RARSfest — Raleigh, NC

NC State Fairgrounds, Raleigh, NC · rarsfest.org · hosting the ARRL Roanoke Division Convention in 2027

Elmer's take — the best fit

The best fit on this list, and it's in our backyard. Indoor, climate-controlled, and you can drive right up to your table to load in. 230+ flea tables, and a lot of buyers come specifically for used gear. Zero travel cost. It sold out in 2024 — register the day registration opens.

Flea market: 230+ tables, large indoor flea market + dealer area
Tables: $25 each + $25 for 120V power (2026)
Admission: Separate from table fee (check current pricing)
Drive from Raleigh: Local — zero travel cost

Worth the gas: There is no gas. This is the one you do not miss.

Southeastern Virginia Regional Hamfest & Swap Meet

Knights of Columbus Hall, 12742 Nettles Dr., Newport News, VA · trcihamfest.com

Elmer's take

Tailgating is five bucks a vehicle, so it costs almost nothing to try. Smaller crowd, about three hours away — I'd rank it as a worthwhile summer add-on rather than a core show.

Flea market: Indoor exhibit hall plus tailgating
Tailgating: $5 per vehicle (2026)
Admission: $10 at the door (2026)
Drive from Raleigh: ~3 hours
September 3–4, 2027 · Labor Day weekend (Fri–Sat)

Shelby Hamfest

Cleveland County Fairgrounds, 1751 E. Marion St., Shelby, NC · shelbyhamfest.com

Elmer's take

The largest used-gear crowd in the radius, and used radio gear is a big part of this show's reputation. Plan on two to three days of travel and lodging — the show runs Friday and Saturday, setup starts Thursday. The main flea market is outdoors in the midway, so bring a canopy and a rain plan for sensitive equipment. There are covered spaces connected to Weathers Arena that would protect the radios — email hamfestshelby@gmail.com to ask about those.

Flea market: Huge outdoor swapmeet + indoor vendor buildings (60+ years running)
Spaces: $20 each, first come first served; Sunday "land rush" before the show (2026)
Admission: $10 at the gate, 12 & under free; free parking (2026)
Drive from Raleigh: ~3 hours + lodging

Worth the gas: Yes — the biggest used-gear turnout on this list. Budget the hotel.
