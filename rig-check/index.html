The Rig Check

Every model has three or four questions that decide what it's worth. Generic listings never ask them. This does.

Everybody tries to hide. But if you answer all the questions and you've truly got a nice radio, the answers let it demand a good price. Leave them blank, and the rig prices itself at the "unknown" discount — fair, but lower. Either way, nobody gets fooled.

Sellers: pick your model, answer honestly, and get a condition report you can paste into any listing anywhere. Buyers: the same questions are your checklist — if a listing can't answer them, price it like the answer is no.

Build your condition report



 


Answers are seller-reported and printed exactly as given — including the blanks. An unanswered question shows as "Not answered." That's deliberate: it's what makes a fully answered report worth something.
