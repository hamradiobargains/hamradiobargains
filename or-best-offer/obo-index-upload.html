Or Best Offer

Every listing here was advertised with two magic words: "or best offer." The seller has already told you the asking price is negotiable — what's missing is the number. So we ran it: each card shows the exact offer range that would make the listing an around-25%-below bargain, figured from genuine recorded sales. The seller invited offers. We did the math.

How Or Best Offer works

  • The listing said the words: every entry here was advertised "or best offer" (or the same invitation in other words). No invitation, no entry — there's nothing to negotiate without one.
  • Priced close to market: the asking price is no more than about 5% above fair market value, figured from genuine recorded sales. A listing priced way over market doesn't make this page, OBO or not — the offer would be a fantasy, and we don't publish fantasies.
  • We name the offer: the range on every card lands around 25% below fair value — the same bar our regular deals have to clear. The top of the range is the ceiling; above it, it stops being a bargain.
  • No promises: a listing here is not a bargain at the asking price, and we can't promise any seller accepts any offer. The math is ours; the negotiating is yours. Be polite, be fair — and do your own homework on the seller, same as anywhere online.

Fair values come from genuine recorded sales in our comp ledger. Or Best Offer listings live on this page only.
