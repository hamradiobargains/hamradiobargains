About the FlexRadio Flex-6600

The FlexRadio FLEX-6600 is a high-performance HF/50 MHz software-defined radio (SDR) in the FLEX-6000 series, designed for advanced amateur and professional operators. It offers direct digital sampling, expanded receiver capabilities, and integrated features for both standalone and remote operation.

Common questions

Can the FLEX-6600 be used for maritime mobile service?

Yes. FlexRadio confirms the FLEX-6600 can be used for maritime mobile service, provided it is registered and operated in compliance with the relevant maritime regulations.

What is the main difference between the FLEX-6400 and FLEX-6600?

The FLEX-6600 adds a balanced audio input on the rear panel (in addition to the standard mic/PTT ports) and is designed for certain commercial/maritime applications where balanced audio is required. Otherwise, the FLEX-6400 and FLEX-6600 share the same core DSP, frequency range, and SmartSDR compatibility.
