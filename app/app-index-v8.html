Put it on your home screen

On an iPhone, in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The HR Bargains icon lands on your home screen and opens full-screen, like any app.

On Android, in Chrome:

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  2. Tap Install app (or “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Confirm. The icon lands on your home screen.
