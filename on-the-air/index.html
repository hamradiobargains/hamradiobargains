The Full Month

All dates verified for 2026 where noted

Oct 31 – Nov 1

ARRL EME Contest

Moonbounce weekend for VHF/UHF weak-signal operators, 50 through 1296 MHz — signals bounced off the moon, worked on the echo.

Oct 31 – Nov 1

UK/EI DX Contest, SSB

Stations across the UK and Ireland work the world on HF phone, Saturday into Sunday.

Oct 31 – Nov 1

Russian WW MultiMode Contest

A worldwide weekend across CW, phone, and digital, running alongside the UK/EI event.

Nov 1

North American SSB Sprint Contest

A fast four-hour phone sprint on Sunday evening — work a station, then move off the frequency and let them call.

Nov 1

High Speed Club CW Contest

Three hours of fast CW on Sunday, run by the High Speed Club — listen first, jump in when you’re ready.

Nov 1–4

Classic Exchange, CW

Vintage rigs on the air for four days — older gear gets bonus credit, and the pace is unhurried.

Nov 3

ARS Spartan Sprint

A two-hour Tuesday-evening QRP sprint — light radios and low power score best.

Nov 7

IPARC Contest, CW

The International Police Association radio club’s CW session, Saturday.

Nov 7–8

YBDXPI FT8 Contest

A full weekend of FT8 digital contacts on HF, Saturday through Sunday.

Nov 7–9

ARRL Sweepstakes Contest, CW

The biggest domestic contest of the fall: work US and Canadian stations on CW, Saturday afternoon to late Sunday night — the goal is a “clean sweep” of all 85 sections.

Nov 8

IPARC Contest, SSB

The phone half of the police-radio club’s weekend, Sunday.

Nov 8

FIRAC HF Contest

The railway radio operators’ HF phone contest, Sunday — a small, friendly event.

Nov 8

4 States QRP Group Second Sunday Sprint

Two hours of low-power operating on Sunday evening — small radios, simple antennas, relaxed pace.

Nov 14–15

WAE DX Contest, RTTY

Worked All Europe on RTTY: stations outside Europe trade message traffic with European stations all weekend.

Nov 14–15

JIDX Phone Contest

Work Japanese stations on HF phone — Japan works the world, Saturday into Sunday.

Nov 14–15

OK/OM DX Contest, CW

Work Czech and Slovak stations on CW — a well-run European CW weekend.

Nov 14–15

Kentucky QSO Party

Hunt Kentucky counties on phone and CW, Saturday afternoon into Saturday night.

Nov 14–15

SKCC Weekend Sprintathon

Straight keys, bugs, and cootie keys only — the Straight Key Century Club’s monthly weekend.

Nov 14–16

CQ-WE Contest

The Western Electric contest — a long-running, laid-back event on phone, CW, and digital, HF through UHF.

Nov 14–16

AWA Bruce Kelley 1929 QSO Party

First of two weekends: transmitters built to 1929-or-earlier designs take over 160, 80, 40, and 20 meters — chirps, drift, and all.

Nov 14–16

PODXS 070 Club Triple Play Low Band Sprint

Three nights of PSK31 on 160, 80, and 40 meters — digital on the low bands after dark.

Nov 15

Homebrew and Oldtime Equipment Party

Home-built and vintage gear on 80 and 40 meters, Sunday — the older the radio, the better it fits.

Nov 15–16

Run for the Bacon QRP Contest

The Flying Pig QRP club’s monthly two-hour sprint, Sunday evening.

Nov 21–22

LZ DX Contest

Work Bulgarian stations and the world on phone and CW, Saturday into Sunday.

Nov 21–23

ARRL Sweepstakes Contest, SSB

The phone half of Sweepstakes: the same 85-section hunt as the CW weekend, on voice this time, Saturday afternoon to late Sunday night.

Nov 21–23

NA Collegiate ARC Championship, SSB

College club stations compete inside the Sweepstakes weekend — work the school stations.

Nov 21–23

AWA Bruce Kelley 1929 QSO Party

Second weekend for the 1929-style transmitters — if you missed the first round, the old rigs are back.

Nov 21

All Austrian 160-Meter Contest

An evening on 160 meters with Austrian stations — top band only, Saturday night.

Nov 21–22

South American Integration Contest, CW

South American stations work the world on CW, Saturday into Sunday.

Nov 25

SKCC Sprint

The Straight Key Century Club’s monthly two-hour evening sprint, the Wednesday before Thanksgiving.

Nov 28–29

CQ WW DX Contest, CW

The CW half of the CQ World Wide: 48 hours of worldwide CW DX on 160–10 meters, the last full weekend of November. The bands will be busy from end to end — a good weekend to get on and work what you hear. Logs are due December 1 at 2359 UTC.

Not into contests? The Classic Exchange (Nov 1–4) and the Bruce Kelley 1929 QSO Party (Nov 14–16 and 21–23) are built around old rigs and unhurried contacts — no sprint pace, just get on and talk about the gear. And in between the contest weekends, the bands are open for ordinary contacts all month. The bands are for everyone.
