Weekend Spotlight

October 24–25, 2026 · CQ WW DX Contest, SSB

October 31–November 1: Moonbounce Weekend, and Europe on Phone

The loudest thing in the sky this weekend isn’t on HF at all — it’s the moon. The ARRL EME Contest runs Friday night (October 30, 8 PM Eastern) through Sunday evening: stations on 50 MHz all the way up to 1296 MHz bounce their signals off the moon and work each other on the echo, a half-second round trip of nearly half a million miles. You don’t need a dish farm to take part — the biggest moonbounce stations are loud enough to hear on a modest VHF or UHF setup, and plenty of operators spend the weekend just listening to the moon.

Down on HF, the UK/EI DX Contest runs on phone from Saturday 8 AM Eastern to Sunday 7 AM: stations across the UK and Ireland working the world, and the world working them. The Russian WW MultiMode contest runs alongside it for the CW and digital crowd. Neither one asks anything of you — no log to submit, no score to keep. Answer a CQ, trade a signal report, move on.

The seasonal bonus

Saturday night runs an hour long. Daylight saving time ends Sunday at 2 AM, so the clocks fall back in the middle of the night — and a longer night is exactly what 80 and 160 meters want. The winter low-band season that started last weekend keeps building from here through December: every week, the low bands get a little better after dark.

The takeaway

A between-weekend, and a good one: moon echoes up high, Europe on phone down low, and an extra hour of dark. And it’s the warm-up — next weekend, Saturday November 7, the CW half of ARRL Sweepstakes opens, the biggest domestic contest of the fall. Get the station sorted now.

Sources: ARRL EME Contest, Oct 30–Nov 1, 2026, 50–1296 MHz; UK/EI DX Contest, SSB, 1200Z Oct 31–1200Z Nov 1, 2026 (WA7BNM Contest Calendar); daylight saving time ends Sun Nov 1, 2026, 2 AM local; ARRL Sweepstakes CW opens Sat Nov 7, 2026.
