About the FlexRadio 6400

The FLEX-6400 is designed for the ham who is looking to operate the radio from a PC, laptop, Maestro, Mac or iOS client - either local or remote.

Common questions

What is the FLEX-6400 and what is it used for?

The FLEX-6400 is a software-defined HF transceiver from FlexRadio Systems, designed for amateur radio and other RF applications. It uses SmartSDR software to provide flexible, high-performance radio operation.

What are the key specifications?

It supports HF bands, offers multiple transmit power levels, and includes both transmitter and receiver sections.

How do I connect the FLEX-6400 to a PC?

You can connect via Ethernet (direct link-local or via a local network) or USB 2.0. The manual provides cabling requirements and setup steps for SmartSDR installation.
