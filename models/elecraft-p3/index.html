About the Elecraft P3

The Elecraft P3 is a high-performance panadapter built for the Elecraft K3 and K3S — a matching-width box that takes the radio's IF output and draws the band as a real-time spectrum and waterfall display, 2 kHz to 200 kHz of span, with sensitivity Elecraft rates down to the K3's own noise floor. It needs no PC for normal operation: it integrates with the K3/K3S in minutes, shows VFO A and B cursors with their bandwidths, and lets you press the SELECT knob to QSY straight to a signal you've spotted. Averaging and peak-hold modes pull weak signals out of the noise visually, and a buffered IF output can drive other equipment such as CW Skimmer. It can also work with other receivers that provide an IF output between 455 kHz and 21.7 MHz, but the K3 pairing — point-and-click tuning, shared function keys, firmware that talks both ways — is what it was designed for. Our recorded sales put fair value around $549.99 on 9 in-window sales, so a genuine bargain sits under about $412. The used-price story on a P3 is the options, exactly as it is on the K3 itself. The P3SVGA adapter is the big one: it adds an external-monitor output (up to 1920×1080), a higher-resolution FFT display, a USB keyboard/data-terminal mode for sending CW/RTTY/PSK text, and flash-drive screen capture — and ledger sales show SVGA-fitted units at the top of the range ($639.99–$695) against plain units at $350–$579. The later Transmit Monitor option is the second divider. A plain P3 at $550 and an SVGA P3 at $550 are not the same deal at all. Buying used, establish the configuration before the price conversation: SVGA fitted or not (look for the EXT DISP and keyboard connectors it adds at the rear), Transmit Monitor fitted or not, and the hookup cables included — ledger sales repeat "with cables" for a reason, and K3/P3 owners may also need the CBLP3Y cable for the KIO3B I/O upgrade. Two compatibility checks from Elecraft's own manual: K3s built before September 2009 want the IF buffer gain modification (a single resistor, ~10 dB more IF output) for best weak-signal display, unless the RF board is version H3 or later; and powering a P3 *with* SVGA from the K3's rear 12V OUT jack requires the jack labelled 1.0 A MAX SWITCHED — older K3s limited to 500 mA need the K312MDKT mod kit, or run the P3 from its own supply. Confirm the display is bright and complete and the firmware loads (current production firmware is MCU 1.60 / SVGA 1.34) before money moves.

Common questions

"Do I need to have the SVGA option installed to use the TX Monitor?"

This is verbatim from Elecraft's own P3 FAQ. The Transmit Monitor is a separate option from the P3SVGA video/FFT adapter — Elecraft's FAQ treats them as independent options with their own install and firmware questions, so establish which options a used unit actually has rather than assuming one implies the other.

"How does the P3 monitor my transmit signal?"

Also Elecraft's own FAQ wording. With the Transmit Monitor option fitted, the P3 samples the transmitted signal through its coupler and displays TX metering and signal-envelope monitoring on the P3 screen (and, per Elecraft's FAQ list, the transmit waveform can also be shown on an external SVGA monitor). Elecraft's firmware notes add that TX sensing at the coupler can be enabled or disabled (TxSensEn) — K3/K3S users can leave it disabled and let the serial connection report TX state.

"Will I be able to see the transmit waveform on an SVGA monitor, too?"

Elecraft's own FAQ again — the P3SVGA option exists precisely to put the P3's displays, including transmit monitoring, on a large external monitor at up to 1920×1080, with the higher-resolution FFT and keyboard data-terminal features alongside. If big-screen operation is why you're buying, an SVGA-less unit doesn't do the job at any price.
