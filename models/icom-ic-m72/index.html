About the Icom IC-M72

The IC-M72 VHF Marine Transceiver is designed for ease of use with a compact, ergonomic body and side grips. It offers high power transmission, impressive audio output, and robust water resistance. Frequency Range: Transmit 156.025-157.425 MHz, Receive 156.050-163.275 MHz.

Common questions

Can I use the BC210 with the BC123S from an M73 to charge my M72?

Yes. Icom confirms that the BC210 charger (with BC123S) can be used to charge the M72's battery.

Is the IC-M72 compatible with other Icom chargers?

Yes, it can use Icom's BC210 charger with BC123S, and the M73 can use the BC166 + BC245 from an M72.

How do I set up the scan function on the IC-M72?

Use the scan operation menu to select your desired scan type (e.g., channel list, TAG channels), start the scan, and adjust the squelch level for optimal reception.
