What Icom announced

After a summer of teasing a locked display case codenamed X-026 — shown at Dayton Hamvention, Ham Radio Friedrichshafen, and WRTC 2026 with no specs and no name — Icom pulled the covers off at Tokyo Ham Fair on August 29, 2026. The X-026 is the IC-7110, the direct successor to the IC-7100 and Icom's first serious update to its all-band mobile line in over a decade.

From Icom's pre-release materials, the headline features:

  • All-band, all-mode: HF, 50 MHz, 144 MHz, and 430 MHz (plus 70 MHz on UK/European models)
  • Power: 100 W on HF/50 MHz, 50 W on 144/430 MHz
  • 4.3-inch color touchscreen with real-time spectrum scope, waterfall, and audio scope
  • Separable slanted control head with built-in speaker, SD card slot, and front mic jack — body mounts elsewhere in the vehicle or shack
  • Dualwatch across HF/VHF and HF/UHF, including D-STAR digital voice dualwatch
  • Modern connectivity: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.3, built-in GNSS (GPS), USB-C, CW decoder, FT8 preset, RS-BA1 remote operation

What Icom has not said: the price, or when it ships. Dealers are taking pre-order deposits with final pricing and delivery dates marked "to be confirmed."

Common questions

When will the Icom IC-7110 ship?

Icom hasn't announced a release date. It was unveiled as pre-release information on August 29, 2026 — specifications and timing can still change before production.

How much will the IC-7110 cost?

Not announced. UK dealers are taking £250 pre-order deposits with final pricing to be confirmed. Anyone quoting you a firm price today is guessing.

Is the IC-7100 discontinued?

Icom positions the IC-7110 as the direct successor to the IC-7100, its long-running all-band mobile rig. If you own a 7100, nothing about your radio changes — but the used market is where the action will be.

Buy on the math, not the hype. A clean used IC-7100 at $600 or less clears the 25% rule today — that's a buy-right price with forty years of logic behind it. The IC-7110 will be new-retail money whenever it ships, and there's no date yet.
