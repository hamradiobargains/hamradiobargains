About the Elecraft KX2

The Elecraft KX2 is a pocket-sized, 80-10m SSB/CW/Data transceiver that delivers full-featured HF performance in a 13 oz, 5.8 x 2.8 x 1.5 in package.

Common questions

What is the Elecraft KX2?

The KX2 is a pocket-sized, 80-10m SSB/CW/data transceiver designed for portable, mobile, and hand-held operation. It weighs only 13 oz, covers 9 HF bands, and can output up to 10W (or 100W with the optional KXPA100 amplifier).

What are the main features?

The KX2 includes dual watch, PSK/RTTY modes, digital voice recorder, stereo audio, noise reduction, multiple audio effects, programmable function switches, and a built-in automatic antenna tuner (KXAT2) option.

What accessories are available?

Common options include the KXPD2 keyer paddle, KXAT2 internal antenna tuner, KXIBC2 battery charger, and KXPA100 amplifier.
